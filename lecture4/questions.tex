\begin{frame}{Question}
    \textit{How can we implement new habits in order to improve health care? (systematic changes)} \pause \vspace{0.5cm}\\
    Habits are hard to change through information and willpower. People are \textbf{present-biased} and act on routine. Lasting change usually comes from changing the environment in which decisions are made $\implies$ The healthy option must become the default, the cheaper one, or the easier one.
\begin{enumerate}\small
  \item Prices: sugar and alcohol taxation for example
  \item Regulation: mandatory warning labels, bans of certain additives or chemicals
  \item Defaults: mainly vegetarian food in cafeterias, opt-out organ donation instead of active consent cards 
\end{enumerate}
\end{frame}
